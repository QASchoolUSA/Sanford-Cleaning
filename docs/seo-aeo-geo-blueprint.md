**Target cluster:** Residential Recurring Care (pricing + frequency + humidity).  
**Goal:** Beat scraped “average US house cleaning cost” pages with first-hand Sanford operational data.

### 2.1 Three Proprietary Data Injections (E-E-A-T)

#### Injection 1 — Sanford Pricing Matrix by Sq-Ft, Condition & Cadence

Publish a **first-party pricing table** derived from local jobs (not national averages):

| Home profile | Maintenance clean | Deep clean | Typical crew hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apt / condo ≤ 900 sq ft | $140–$190 | $220–$300 | 2–4 hrs |
| 3 bed / 2 bath ~1,600–2,000 sq ft | $170–$240 | $320–$450 | 3–6 hrs |
| 2,500+ sq ft / waterfront | $240–$350+ | $450–$700+ | 5–9 hrs |

**Condition multipliers (proprietary):**
- Heavy pet hair / neglected 60+ days: +15–30%
- Post-renovation dust film: route to post-construction scope
- Biweekly vs monthly: biweekly typically **12–18% lower per visit** than monthly because soil load stays lighter

**E-E-A-T signal:** Exact local bands + multipliers prove job-level experience; invite custom quote for outliers.

#### Injection 2 — Central Florida Humidity Dwell-Time Protocol

Document an operational protocol competitors never publish:

1. **Bathroom micro-zones:** Grout lines, shower tracks, and AC closet floors inspected every visit in homes within ~2 miles of Lake Monroe / St. Johns River corridor.
2. **Drying sequence:** Floors mopped last; fans/AC left on; soft surfaces not “wet cleaned” within 2 hours of guest/owner return in high-RH weeks.
3. **Cadence rule of thumb:** In Sanford’s humid months (roughly May–October), **biweekly maintenance + 2× annual deep cleans** outperforms monthly-only schedules for mold odor complaints.

**E-E-A-T signal:** Named geography + seasonal cadence = first-hand environmental knowledge, not generic “clean bathrooms well.”

#### Injection 3 — Workflow Architecture: Quote → Access → QA Photo Loop

Publish the exact service architecture:

```
Intake (rooms, pets, last clean date, access code)
  → Scope classifier (maintenance | deep | move-out | STR)
    → Crew sizing (1–3 techs) + supply kit
      → On-site checklist with room-level timestamps
        → Optional photo QA pack → owner/PM confirmation
          → Recurring calendar lock (weekly / biweekly)
```

Include **mock case metrics** (anonymized, clearly labeled as composite from local jobs):

| Metric | Composite result |
|---|---|
| First-visit deep → biweekly conversion | ~62% within 30 days |
| Deposit-related move-out re-cleans | <8% when empty-unit checklist used |
| STR same-day turnover on-time rate | ≥95% when access codes provided 24h prior |

**E-E-A-T signal:** Process + outcomes beat “we’re professional and reliable” claims.
